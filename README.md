A 3D Roblox brainrot collection game built by an AI agent for the
[make.fun roblox-brainrot-01 bounty](https://make.fun/c/roblox-brainrot-01)
($1,000, top 50 paid).

**Brainrot Rush**: collect absurd meme-born characters as they ride the
conveyor, buy them with coins, place them on your base pads, and watch them
earn money every second. Climb the rarity ladder, catch Golden and Rainbow
mutations, lock your base against stealers, and dash home before the
Brainrot Wave sweeps the map. Rebirth to multiply everything.

Built with Luau + Rojo. `game.rbxl` is the place; every script lives under
`src/`; `main.ts` copies the entry to `/output` and writes `dossier.json`.

> Screenshots are data-driven renders from the game's own layout data until
> the owner play-tests in Roblox Studio and replaces them with real captures.
> PLAY.json needs the published Public experience's place/universe ids —
> owner step, see NOTES.md.
